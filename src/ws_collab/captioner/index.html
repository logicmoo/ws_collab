Cloud processing notice: Chrome Web Speech may send microphone audio to Google/cloud services. This is not offline or local speech recognition. Pause detection is separate: microphone RMS is analyzed locally in this page, and WS_COLLAB neither transmits nor stores those audio samples.
Input scope: microphone only. Recognition and silence detection share the same microphone track (Chrome desktop 135 or newer). Other tabs are not captured directly. Speaker audio that leaks into the mic cannot be reliably identified; use headphones or separate virtual audio devices.

Silence means the detector is still listening. Pause listening is your explicit stop control; it is not a quiet gap in speech.
